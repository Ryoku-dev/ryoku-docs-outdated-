## Your card

Your card shows your computer: its name, its picture and how strong it is. Strong hardware makes a rarer card. Built-in graphics count too.

Open **Settings › Home › Customise** to change the picture and words, then **Share** to copy or save the card as a picture.

## Your friends' cards

Send your card picture to a friend. They paste it into their **Binder** (Settings › Home › Binder) and your card appears there. The rarest cards sit at the top.

## Keep my card up to date (optional)

In **Customise › Share** there is a switch: **Keep my card up to date for friends**.

| | Off | On |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your card is | the picture you sent | the picture you sent, and a live copy online |
| You change your picture or words | friends see the change only if you send a new picture | friends see the change the next time their binder checks |

- Only your own computer can change your card. Nobody else can.
- If you don't change your card for **3 months**, it is taken offline. Friends who already have it keep the last version they got.
- Turning the switch off takes your card offline too. Friends keep the copy they have.

## When binders check

A binder looks for changes now and then, not all the time: about every 15 minutes, and every time it opens for badge holders. It shows when it last checked and when the next check is. On busy days the card service can pause until the next day; your binder says so and keeps showing your cards as they were.

## Badges

Testers, contributors and the Ryoku team get a badge on their card.

1. Open **Settings › Home › Customise › Badge** and copy your request code.
2. In the Ryoku Discord, go to **#claim-badge** and type `/badge` with your GitHub name and the code.
3. Paste the badge code you get back into the Badge tab and press **Activate**.

A badge works only on the computer that asked for it. Using another computer? Ask again from that one.
